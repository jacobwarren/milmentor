Privacy policy

Your hunts stay
on your phone.

Mil Mentor has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I don't run a server, so nothing you do in the app is sent to me.

Who makes Mil Mentor

Mil Mentor is made by Jacob Warren, an independent developer. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean Jacob Warren, and "the app" means Mil Mentor for iPhone.

What the app keeps, and where

Your rifle and load, your settings, your shot history, ratings and streak, the places you scout and your shot calls are saved on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They're included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud or your computer) like any app's data, and deleting the app deletes them. I can't see any of it.

Location

The app asks for your location only for Shot Call, and only while you're using it. It saves where you shot from, where you last saw the animal and each spot of blood you mark, and it uses the compass for direction. All of that stays on your phone unless you share a shot report. The app never follows your location in the background.

You can turn location off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Mil Mentor.

What leaves your phone, and to whom

A few features need data from outside services. The app talks to them directly, and nothing passes through me.

Apple Maps

The satellite maps in Shot Call and in the place picker come from Apple. When you drop a pin on a place, or mark a shot, the app sends that spot's coordinates to Apple's geocoder to get its name or state. Apple's privacy policy covers these requests.

Elevation data

When you scout a place, the app downloads elevation tiles for that area from the public Terrain Tiles dataset hosted by Amazon Web Services. Like any download, Amazon sees your IP address and which tiles were asked for, which shows roughly where the place is. The request carries nothing else about you, and the tiles are kept on your phone afterwards. See Amazon's privacy notice.

Purchases

Real Terrain is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the app only learns whether you own it. I never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the Shot call shortcut, Siri and the Shortcuts app handle your request under Apple's terms.

Notifications

The daily shot reminder and Shot Call's "time to track" alert are scheduled on your phone. There's no push server, and you can turn them off in Settings → Notifications → Mil Mentor.

When you share

Nothing is shared unless you tap Share and pick where it goes.

  • A shot card shows where the shot hit and the conditions. It never names a real place you've scouted.
  • A Shot Call report includes Apple Maps links with the exact spots where you shot from, where you last saw the animal and the last blood. Send it only to people you'd want on the trail.

The people you send it to, and the app you send it with, have their own privacy terms.

Links to other sites

Shot Call links to United Blood Trackers, the TRAKR app's App Store page and Apple Maps. Each has its own privacy policy.

What Apple may share with me

  • If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple gives me combined statistics, like how many people opened the app, and crash reports. These don't identify you.
  • If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shows me the name and email you were invited with (testers who join from a public link stay anonymous), how often the beta is opened, your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback and screenshots you send. I use these only to fix and improve the app.

Email

If you email me, I use your address and what you wrote only to reply, and I don't share them with anyone.

Children

Mil Mentor isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Turn location or notifications off at any time in Settings.
  • Profile → Reset progress clears your ratings and shot history.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it saved on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app ever starts sending your data anywhere new, this page will say so before that update ships.
