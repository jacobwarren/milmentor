Common questions

How do I start a shot call fast?

Tap Just shot one? on Home, or say "Shot call in Mil Mentor" to Siri. You can also put the Shot call shortcut on your Action button (Settings → Action Button → Shortcut, then Mil Mentor's Shot call) or on your Lock Screen.

Is Shot Call's wait right for my deer?

Shot Call is a guide built from blood trackers' rules of thumb. It can't see the deer. When in doubt, wait longer, and call a tracker if the trail runs out. The trail screen has a link to find a tracker near you.

Shot Call can't find where I am.

Turn location on for Mil Mentor in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Mil Mentor, choose While Using the App, and leave Precise Location on. Under thick timber or in a deep draw, give the GPS a moment to settle.

I didn't get a reminder or the "time to track" alert.

Check that notifications are on in Settings → Notifications → Mil Mentor, and that a Focus mode isn't holding them. The daily shot reminder is switched on in Profile.

How accurate are the ballistics?

Mil Mentor is a practice tool. Its ballistics are a model: confirm your own dope at the range before you hunt, and know your state's regulations.

How do I get Real Terrain on a new phone?

Sign in with the Apple Account you bought it with, then tap Profile → Restore purchase.

Real Terrain says the store isn't available.

Check your connection and try again. If Screen Time limits in-app purchases on your phone, the purchase can't start until it's allowed. With Ask to Buy, Real Terrain unlocks once the family organizer approves it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I start over?

Profile → Reset progress clears your ratings and shot history. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

What does Mil Mentor do with my data?

It keeps it on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Read the privacy policy for the details.
